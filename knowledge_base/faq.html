Orbitly Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 10 November 2025

Account and sign-in

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We will send you a link to choose a new password.

Why is my account locked?

For your security, your account is locked for 30 minutes after 3 failed sign-in attempts. Wait for the lock to expire or reset your password.

Does Orbitly support single sign-on?

Yes. SAML single sign-on is available on the Enterprise plan. See the SSO Configuration Guide for setup instructions.

Billing

Can I get a refund?

Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are non-refundable.

How much storage do I get?

Storage depends on your plan: Basic workspaces get 10 GB, Pro workspaces get 100 GB and Enterprise workspaces get 1 TB.

Apps and browsers

Can I use Orbitly on my phone?

Yes. Orbitly apps are available for iOS and Android. Download them from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with your work account.

Which browsers are supported?

The latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Contacting support

Use the Help button in the app or email support@orbitly.example. Response times depend on your plan.
