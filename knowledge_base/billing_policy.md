# Billing and Plans Policy

This policy describes Orbitly plans, prices, invoices, refunds and support levels. It replaces all earlier billing policies.

## Plans and pricing

| Plan | Price | Billing options |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Basic | $12 per user per month | Monthly or annual |
| Pro | $29 per user per month | Monthly or annual |
| Enterprise | $55 per user per month (minimum 50 users) | Annual only |

Annual billing receives a 15% discount on the listed price. Guests are free and are not counted as paid users.

## Payment methods

Orbitly accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit cards. Annual Enterprise subscriptions can also be paid by bank transfer against an invoice.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each billing cycle. Past invoices can be downloaded as PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoices. To change the billing contact or add a tax ID, open Settings > Billing > Details.

## Upgrading your plan

Upgrades take effect immediately, so new features are available as soon as the upgrade is confirmed. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle, and unused time on the previous plan is credited against that charge.

## Downgrading your plan

Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. You keep the features of your current plan until then, and no credit is issued for the current cycle.

## Refunds

### Monthly subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing cycle.

### Annual subscriptions

Annual subscriptions can be refunded on a prorated basis if they are cancelled within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable.

### Mid-cycle upgrades and refunds

If you upgrade in the middle of a billing cycle, the prorated upgrade charge on a monthly subscription is non-refundable. On an annual subscription, a refund requested within the 30-day window is calculated on the upgraded plan price for the unused days.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Orbitly retries the charge 3 times over 14 days and emails the billing contact after each attempt. If payment is still outstanding after the 14-day grace period, the workspace becomes read-only until the balance is paid.

## Cancellation

To cancel, the Owner opens Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Access continues until the end of the paid period. Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation; see the Administrator Handbook for details.

## Support plans and response times

| Plan | Support channels | First response target |
|------|------------------|-----------------------|
| Basic | Email | within 48 hours |
| Pro | Email and live chat | within 24 hours |
| Enterprise | 24/7 phone, email and chat, named customer success manager | within 4 hours for critical issues |
