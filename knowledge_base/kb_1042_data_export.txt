HOW TO EXPORT YOUR WORKSPACE DATA
Support article KB-1042
Last updated: 2026-02-10

WHO CAN EXPORT

Only the Owner and Admins can export workspace data.

STARTING AN EXPORT

Open Settings > Workspace > Export and choose what to include. Exports are available in CSV and JSON formats. Exports include projects, tasks, comments and member lists. Files and attachments are exported separately as a ZIP archive.

DOWNLOADING LARGE EXPORTS

Small exports download immediately. Large exports are prepared in the background and the download link is sent by email. The download link is valid for 7 days.
